About This Website

This website provides independently listed resale ticket information for selected events.

Listings may include ticket type, section, row range, quantity, date, and seller-provided resale price.

How Ticket Requests Work

  1. Browse the available listings on the Tickets page.
  2. Select the ticket listing you are interested in.
  3. Use the request button to contact the seller by email.
  4. Confirm availability, seating information, transfer details, and final terms with the seller.
  5. Only proceed with a purchase after you are satisfied with the ticket information and transaction terms.
